How to use with IONOS:
1. Log into your IONOS account.
2. Open Website Builder or Hosting File Manager.
3. Upload all files into your website root directory.
4. Replace placeholder phone number and email.
5. Connect your contact form to IONOS Forms or email forwarding.
6. Integrate tracking software:
   - Google Maps
   - FleetComplete
   - Samsara
